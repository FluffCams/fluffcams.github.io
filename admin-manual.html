Admin manual

Everything about the site is changed in its admin panel, and every admin page has a Manual button that opens the manual at the entry for that page. The manual is organised the same way as the admin menu.

Start here and the first day

How the admin panel is laid out, who on your team can open which page, and a checklist for the first day of a new site: your name and logo, colours, payment providers, email, the live video connection and your legal pages.

People

Members, models, studios and performers in couple and group accounts; new model applications and their ID checks; who is online right now; and accounts for the people on your team, each with their own permissions.

Live room

The live video connection and room settings, interactive toys, reactions and sounds, how long visitors may watch before signing up, Reels, paid room promotion, ticket shows, viewer cameras and room statistics.

Browse

Categories, the left menu and the strip under the header, home page sections, the profile details visitors can filter by, page headings for search engines, and the designer for the model cards in the grid.

Token sales, refunds and payouts

Orders and token packages, the payment providers you connect with your own accounts, coupons, tax and receipts, chargebacks and refunds, and paying models and studios by PayPal, bank transfer, Paxum or crypto, by hand or on a schedule.

Affiliates and messaging

Your own affiliate program with links, banners and payouts, and the private messaging between members and models, including paid messages and the word filter.

Trust and safety

ID and age verification with the provider you choose, reports, moderation, upload screening, blocked addresses and the legal record keeping for models.

Site and look, marketing and connections

Colours, looks, logos, legal pages and search engine settings; emails, newsletters, automations, promotions, loyalty levels and alerts; and the outside services you connect, such as email, storage, sign-in with Google or X, captcha and translation.

System

Scheduled jobs, backups, system health, visitor statistics, maintenance mode, updates, the license and plugins.

Recipes

Short step-by-step answers for the jobs you do most often, and a list of the words used in the admin panel.
