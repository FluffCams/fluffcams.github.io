FluffCams documentation

FluffCams is software for running your own live webcam site: live rooms, private and group shows, tokens, fan clubs, paid messages, studios and an affiliate program, all managed from one admin panel. These pages give an overview of each guide; the complete guides are on fluffcams.com.

What you get

  • A complete cam site that works on phones and computers: live rooms with chat, tips, tip menus and goals, private, group and ticket shows.
  • Ways for models to earn beyond live shows: fan clubs, paid messages with pay-per-view attachments, photos and videos for sale, albums and booked shows.
  • Token sales with the payment providers you choose, payouts to models and studios, tax, refunds and chargeback handling.
  • Studios that manage their own models, couples and groups on one account, ID and age checks, moderation and reports.
  • Your own look and brand: colours, ready-made looks, a card designer, your logo, your domain and your own pages.
