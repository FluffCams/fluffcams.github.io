Guide for models

Every FluffCams site includes this guide for its models, and each page of a model's account has a Help link that opens it at the right place.

Getting started

The dashboard: what still needs to be done before going live for the first time, today's earnings and the latest activity.

Going live

The Studio runs in the browser on a computer or a phone, or takes the picture from OBS. From it the model goes live, accepts private shows, starts ticket and group shows, and records clips and photos.

Profile and media

The public profile, ID verification, adding a partner or group members to the account, and photos, videos and albums that can be free, for fan club members only, or for sale.

Earning and growing

Paid messages, followers, the fan club, booked shows, promoting the room on the front page, and statistics about viewers and earnings.

Keeping the room in order

Choosing moderators, blocking people from the chat, working with the site team, and hiding the room from chosen countries or regions.

Getting paid

Earnings and payouts, account settings, and which alerts arrive by email or on the phone.
