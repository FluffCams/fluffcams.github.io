Guide for studios

Studios are agencies that manage several models. A model keeps their own account and ID check; the studio sees their rooms and money while they work with it.

The studio dashboard, with the studio's models that are live right now.

Your models

Money

Studio earnings and payouts, with the site paying either the studio alone or the model and the studio their agreed shares; studio details, alerts and the account.
